# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant][cc], version 2.1, in full.
Everyone taking part — in issues, pull requests, discussions or by email — is
expected to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, to be
respectful of differing opinions and experiences, to give and accept
constructive feedback gracefully, and to accept responsibility for mistakes.

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainer at
<johannes.schuster@uni-bamberg.de>. All complaints are reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter is respected.

The full text, including the enforcement guidelines, is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

[cc]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org
